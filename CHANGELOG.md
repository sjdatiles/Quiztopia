# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — Initial Classroom Release
- **Added**: Shared classroom team authentication (2–4 members, team captain, scrypt + HMAC cookie sessions) and Teacher authentication (password + Google Sign-In).
- **Added**: Real-time WebSocket Live Quiz Arena with PIN join, server-enforced countdowns, single locked team submission per question, live teacher host controls, and celebratory results podium.
- **Added**: Fantasy Pet Shop & 2D Island Builder featuring 9 original fantasy creatures, 8 buildings/decorations, atomic coin ledger transactions, and interactive slot placement.
- **Added**: 4-view Leaderboards (Current Term Standings, Last Quiz Results, All-Time Hall of Fame, and Archived Term Champions) plus 8 unlockable team achievements.
- **Added**: Three-term academic season management allowing teachers to close and archive Term 1–3 rankings while preserving lifetime team collections and achievements.
- **Added**: Teacher Quiz Builder, Classroom & Team Roster management, configurable scoring/Island Power rules, and Analytics Reports.
